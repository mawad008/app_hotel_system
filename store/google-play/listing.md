## English (en-US)

**App name** (30 max)

Hotel System

**Short description** (80 max)

Book your stay, check in from your phone and manage everything in one app.

**Full description** (4000 max)

Hotel System is the guest app for our hotel group. Find a hotel, book a room and run your whole stay from your phone.

BROWSE AND BOOK
• Explore the group's hotels with photos, ratings, facilities and location
• Pick your dates and see the rooms available, with clear prices per night and in total
• Browse freely — you only need an account when you confirm a booking

SIGN IN WITH YOUR PHONE NUMBER
• No password to remember: sign in with a one-time code sent to your mobile

CHECK IN WITHOUT THE QUEUE
• Verify your identity once from your phone before you arrive
• Check in from the app and get your room access code

DURING YOUR STAY
• Request room cleaning and room service
• Report a problem and follow it until it is solved
• Extend your stay
• See your extra charges at any time

CHECK OUT AND AFTER
• Check out from the app and receive your invoice
• See your current, upcoming and past bookings
• Collect loyalty points across the group and rate your stay

Available in Arabic and English.
