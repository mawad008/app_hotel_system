# Hotel System — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 7 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the **Hotel System** mobile app
("the app", package `com.hotelsystem.hotel_guest_app`) collects, why, and the
choices you have. The app lets guests browse hotels, book a stay, verify their
identity, check in, use a digital room key, order services and check out.

## Data we collect

| Data | When | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Mobile phone number | When you sign in | To create and secure your account with a one-time code, and to send booking and room-access messages |
| Full name and email address | When you complete your profile | To identify you as the guest on a booking and to send invoices and booking confirmations |
| Photo of your identity document, and a selfie where the hotel requires one | During identity verification before check-in | To confirm the guest's identity, as hotels are required to do by law |
| Booking and stay details (hotel, room, dates, guests, service orders, problem reports, reviews, loyalty points) | When you use those features | To provide the booking and the stay |
| Payment information | When you pay or place a deposit hold | To take payment for your stay. Card details are passed to the payment provider and are **not stored** by the app or our systems |
| App language and onboarding status | When you use the app | To remember your preferences on your device |

The app does not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifier, and
contains no advertising or third-party analytics.

## Device permissions

- **Camera** — used only to photograph your identity document and take the
  selfie during identity verification. The app does not record video or audio
  and does not read your photo library.

## How identity photos are handled

Identity photos are transmitted over an encrypted connection, stored encrypted,
and deleted automatically after your stay ends, subject to any period the hotel
is legally required to keep a copy. They are used only for identity
verification.

## Who we share data with

- **The hotel you book with**, so that it can provide your stay.
- **Service providers acting on our behalf** — hosting, SMS delivery, payment
  processing and identity-document checking — who may only use the data to
  provide that service to us.
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data.

## Security

All communication between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Your sign-in
token is kept in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain)
and is excluded from device backups.

## How long we keep data

We keep account and booking data for as long as your account is active and for
as long as tax, accounting and hospitality regulations require afterwards.
Identity photos are deleted as described above.

## Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your name and email in the app (*Account → My details*),
and request deletion of your personal data in the app (*Account → Privacy &
data*) or by contacting us. Some records may be kept where the law requires it.
You may also ask us for a copy of your data or for it to be corrected.

## Children

The app is not directed at children, and is intended for guests who are old
enough to book a hotel stay.
