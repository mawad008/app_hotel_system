# Hotel System (Stdy Hotel) — Delete your account and data

This page explains how guests of the **Hotel System / Stdy Hotel** app
(package `com.hotelsystem.hotel_guest_app`) can ask for their account and
personal data to be deleted.

## How to request deletion

**In the app**

1. Open the app and sign in.
2. Go to **Account → Privacy & data**.
3. Choose **Delete my personal data** and confirm.

**By email**

Send a message to [ahmedwaleed3378@gmail.com](mailto:ahmedwaleed3378@gmail.com)
from any address, with the subject "Delete my account" and the mobile number you
signed in with. We confirm the request and complete it within 30 days.

## What is deleted

- Your account: mobile number, name and email address
- Identity document photos and selfie, if still held
- Your preferences, favourites and reviews

## What may be kept, and for how long

- Records of completed stays, invoices and payments are kept for as long as tax,
  accounting and hospitality regulations require, then deleted.
- If you have a current or upcoming booking, the request is completed after the
  stay ends or the booking is cancelled.

## Deleting some data without deleting the account

You can edit your name and email in **Account → My details**. Identity photos
are deleted automatically after your stay ends.

See also the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).
